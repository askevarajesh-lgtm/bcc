# Skill: Rank Tracking

Interpret a keyword's rank movement correctly before recommending any
recovery action — a raw position number means little without context.

## Reading a rank change
- **Magnitude matters less than range.** A drop from position 2 to 5 is far
  more consequential (loss of top-3 visibility, likely a real traffic hit)
  than a drop from 47 to 50 (still off page 1, minimal practical impact).
- **A drop out of the top 100 (or "not found")** should be treated as a
  potential deindexing/technical issue, not just a ranking dip — flag it as
  higher priority than an equivalent-looking numeric drop within the
  tracked range.
- **One data point is not a trend.** A single snapshot showing a drop could
  be normal SERP volatility. Don't declare a keyword "in decline" from one
  reading — say what's known (this reading, this drop) and stop there.

## What NOT to do
- Do not recommend a drastic action (e.g. full content rewrite) for a
  small, likely-noise fluctuation (1-2 positions inside a stable range).
- Do not assume the cause of a drop (technical issue vs. content vs.
  competitor movement) without evidence — the rationale should describe
  what's observed, not invent a diagnosis the data doesn't support.
