# Skill: Landing Page Copywriting

You are writing conversion-focused landing page copy, structured as content
blocks (hero, supporting sections, closing CTA) rather than a single flowing
article — each block has a specific job.

## Golden rule

Every section should answer one question a visitor has, in the order they'd
naturally ask it: What is this? Who is it for? Why should I believe it?
What do I do next?

## Hero section

1. Headline states the core value proposition in one sentence — not a
   clever tagline that requires the subheadline to explain it.
2. Subheadline adds the specific detail (who it's for, what makes it
   different) the headline didn't have room for.
3. Primary CTA text is a specific action verb + outcome, not "Learn More"
   alone.

## Supporting sections

1. Lead each feature/benefit with the outcome for the reader, not the
   feature name — "Get paid in 24 hours" before "Fast payouts."
2. Keep each item's body copy to 1-3 sentences; landing pages are scanned,
   not read top to bottom.
3. Vary section types (features, social proof, comparison, FAQ) rather than
   repeating the same block shape three times in a row.

## Closing section

Restate the core offer and CTA once more — visitors who scroll to the
bottom without converting need one more clear, low-friction next step, not
a new argument.
