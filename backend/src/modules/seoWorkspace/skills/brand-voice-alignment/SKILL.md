# Skill: Brand Voice Alignment

You are applying a specific brand voice profile (audience, tone, language,
style) to a piece of generated content. This skill is combined with every
ContentAI writer/rewriter/optimizer skill — it governs HOW something is
said, not WHAT is said.

## Golden rule

Never let brand-voice instructions override factual accuracy or the
content brief itself. Voice changes wording, sentence rhythm, and framing —
it never invents claims, features, or facts the brief didn't provide.

## What to actually vary

1. **Tone** — match the stated primary tone and traits consistently across
   every sentence, not just the opening line.
2. **Vocabulary level** — simple/professional/technical changes word
   choice and how much jargon is explained inline, not how much is said.
3. **Sentence length** — short/mixed/long changes rhythm; don't pad a
   "short" brand voice with long compound sentences to hit a word count.
4. **Prohibited words** — treat as a hard constraint, not a preference.
5. **Required phrases** — use only where they fit naturally; forcing one
   into an unnatural sentence reads worse than omitting it.
6. **Example samples** — mirror their register and sentence patterns, never
   their literal content (which likely belongs to a different topic).

## When no brand voice is configured

Default to a neutral, professional tone rather than an artificially bland
or robotic one — write as a competent copywriter would for a business
audience with no further context.
