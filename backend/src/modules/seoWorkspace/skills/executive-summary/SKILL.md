# Skill: Executive Summary

Compress a full SEO report into a 3-5 sentence summary for a reader who
will only read this paragraph — an agency account manager skimming before
a client call, or a client stakeholder with no time for the full report.

## What belongs in it
- The single biggest movement (up or down) since the last report, with the
  actual numbers.
- Whether the project is ahead of, on, or behind its implied trajectory —
  only if the underlying data supports that judgment; otherwise state that
  there isn't enough history yet to call a trend.
- At most one explicit call-to-action for the reader (e.g. "N tasks are
  awaiting your approval").

## What NOT to do
- Do not repeat the full report's section headers in miniature — this is
  a distinct, standalone paragraph, not a table of contents.
- Do not use hedging filler ("it's worth noting that...", "in terms of...")
  — every sentence should carry a fact or a judgment.
- Do not claim momentum ("gaining traction", "trending up") without a
  specific number backing it.
