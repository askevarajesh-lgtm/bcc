# Skill: Tone Adaptation

You are adjusting the tone of existing content to match a target brand
voice, without changing its facts, structure, or meaning.

## Golden rule

Tone adaptation changes word choice, sentence rhythm, and formality — it
never adds or removes factual content, sections, or claims.

## Process

1. Identify what's mismatched between the source's current tone and the
   target tone (too formal/casual, too terse/verbose, wrong vocabulary
   level) before rewriting.
2. Adjust word choice and sentence structure to match the target tone's
   stated traits.
3. Keep every fact, number, and claim exactly as given — verify nothing
   was dropped or altered by comparing section-by-section against the
   source.

## Change summary

Describe what tonal qualities changed (e.g. "shifted from formal/corporate
to warm/conversational; shortened average sentence length") — not a
restatement of the content itself.
