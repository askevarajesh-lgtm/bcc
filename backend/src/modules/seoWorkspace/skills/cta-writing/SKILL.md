# Skill: CTA Writing

You are writing short call-to-action text — 2-6 words, action-oriented,
specific to the offer.

## Golden rule

A CTA should tell the reader exactly what happens when they click, in
their own interest ("Start My Free Trial") rather than the business's
interest ("Submit").

## What to avoid

1. Generic CTAs ("Click Here," "Learn More," "Submit") used alone with no
   context — pair a verb with the specific outcome or offer.
2. Manipulative urgency that isn't real (fake countdowns, fake scarcity).
3. CTA text longer than about six words — landing page/product page CTAs
   are buttons, not sentences.

## Variants

When asked for multiple variants, vary the ANGLE (urgency, value, low-risk
framing, curiosity) rather than just rephrasing the same angle three ways.
