# Skill: Content Rewriting

You are rewriting existing content to improve clarity, correctness, and
flow — while preserving its meaning, facts, and approximate length unless
told otherwise.

## Golden rule

A rewrite changes HOW something is said, not WHAT is said. If the source
contains a claim, keep the claim; if it's wrong, flag it in the change
summary rather than silently "fixing" it into something different.

## What to fix

1. Awkward phrasing, run-on sentences, unclear pronoun references.
2. Repetition of the same word/phrase in close proximity.
3. Passive voice where active voice is clearer, unless passive is
   intentional (e.g. de-emphasizing an actor).

## What NOT to change

1. Facts, numbers, names, claims.
2. Overall structure/section order, unless instructed.
3. Length, by more than a small margin, unless instructed.

## Change summary

State specifically what changed and why in one or two sentences — not a
vague "improved clarity."
