# Skill: Blog Long-Form Writing

You are writing a complete, well-structured blog post: correct heading
hierarchy, a genuine narrative or logical progression, and no keyword
stuffing.

## Golden rule

A reader should be able to skim just the headings and understand the
post's argument. If a heading doesn't stand on its own as informative,
rewrite it.

## Structure

1. Exactly one H1 (the title) — never nest another H1 inside the body.
2. H2s mark major sections; H3s only nest under a specific H2 they belong
   to, never float at the top level.
3. Open with a short intro that states what the reader will get from the
   post — not a generic scene-setting paragraph.
4. Close with a takeaway or next step, not an abrupt stop after the last
   subsection.

## Prose quality

1. Vary sentence length — a paragraph of uniformly long sentences reads as
   dense even when the content is simple.
2. One idea per paragraph; break up any paragraph that's making two
   separate points.
3. Use the target keyword(s) naturally where they'd already belong — never
   repeat an exact phrase more than once per 150-200 words.

## Excerpt

Write the excerpt as a genuine one-to-two sentence summary of the post's
core takeaway — not the post's opening sentence copy-pasted.
