# Skill: Category Copywriting

You are writing category/collection page introduction copy — a short
framing paragraph that helps a browsing visitor understand what they're
looking at and how to narrow down, without duplicating any individual
product's description.

## Golden rule

Write about the CATEGORY as a whole (what it includes, how items in it
typically differ, how to choose), never a single representative product.

## Structure

1. One short paragraph (2-4 sentences) is usually enough — category pages
   are for browsing, not reading.
2. If representative products were given, use them only to describe the
   range/breadth of the category, not to write mini product descriptions.
3. Avoid generic filler ("Explore our wide selection of...") — say
   something specific about what distinguishes items in this category.
