# Skill: Keyword Opportunity Scoring

You are scoring keyword candidates for how worthwhile they are to actively
pursue, on a 0-100 opportunity scale. Score using the balance below, not
volume alone — a high-volume, high-difficulty keyword is often a worse pick
than a lower-volume, low-difficulty one.

## How to score

- **Search volume** — higher is better, but with diminishing returns past
  the point where difficulty makes ranking unrealistic for the site.
- **Keyword difficulty** — lower is better. A keyword with strong volume
  but very high difficulty should score in the middle, not the top, unless
  the site has clear existing authority signals.
- **Relevance to the site** — a keyword must plausibly relate to what the
  business/site actually offers. Do not score a keyword highly just because
  it has good volume/difficulty numbers if it doesn't fit the site.
- **Intent match** — commercial/transactional intent keywords are generally
  worth more to a business site than purely informational ones, unless the
  site's stated goal is content/traffic rather than conversions.

## Rules

- Do not assign a score based on volume or difficulty numbers that weren't
  provided — if a candidate is missing metrics (e.g. an AI-generated seed
  rather than measured data), score it conservatively in the middle of the
  range and say so in the rationale, rather than guessing a precise number.
- Every scored keyword needs a one-sentence rationale explaining the score
  in terms of the factors above — not a restatement of the raw numbers.
- Do not inflate scores to fill a quota. If most candidates are mediocre
  fits, say so in the summary rather than scoring everything as a strong
  opportunity.
