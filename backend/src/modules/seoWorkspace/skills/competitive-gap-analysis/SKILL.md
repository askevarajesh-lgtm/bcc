# Skill: Competitive Gap Analysis

Identify concrete, actionable gaps between the tracked site and a competitor
— content and positioning gaps a human reviewer can turn into a task, not
generic observations.

## Producing strengths / weaknesses / contentGaps
- **strengths** — what the competitor is objectively doing better,
  grounded in the metrics given (e.g. "significantly larger keyword
  footprint", "stronger backlink authority"). Do not invent qualitative
  claims (design quality, brand trust, UX) that aren't supported by the
  provided data.
- **weaknesses** — where the competitor is objectively behind the tracked
  site on the same metrics, or a specific overlap term where the tracked
  site already outranks them (protect, don't ignore, existing strengths).
- **contentGaps** — specific topic/keyword areas implied by the overlap
  data where the competitor likely has content the tracked site doesn't
  (or vice versa) — phrase each as something a content brief could be
  written from, not a vague theme.

## Keep it comparative, not standalone
Every item should reference the *relationship* between the two domains
(more/less/stronger/weaker than the tracked site), not describe the
competitor in isolation — a reviewer is deciding whether to act relative
to their own site's position, not reading a generic competitor profile.

## Volume discipline
Cap each list (strengths/weaknesses/contentGaps) at the 3-5 most
significant items per competitor. A long list of minor observations is
harder to act on than a short list of the ones that matter most.
